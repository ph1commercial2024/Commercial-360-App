What changes with Option A
Color tokens
Background shifts from warm #F8F7F5 to cool #F2F2F7. Secondary text cools from #6B6860 → #8E8E93.
Cards
Border removed. Box-shadow replaces it. Radius stays at 12px. Section labels appear above grouped cards.
Inputs
Font grows from 12px → 13px. Background becomes #F9F9F9 — makes the field clearly distinct from the card.
Buttons
Gradient drops to flat #ED6055. Border-radius goes from 7px → 20px. Secondary flips to outlined coral instead of gray.
Nav bar
Global nav gains backdrop-filter: blur translucency. Page title grows to 15px / −0.3px tracking.
List separators
Full-width 1px rules become left-indented inset separators (iOS grouped style), with a disclosure chevron on each row.
No structural changes required
Option A updates only the C token object and styles helper at the top of App.jsx. Every page inherits the new look automatically — no component restructuring, no new files.
